
# Privacy Policy for Foreword

**Effective date:** 22 August 2026
**Last updated:** 22 August 2026

Foreword ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is a daily word puzzle game published by
Pen and String, an individual developer based in the Philippines.

This policy explains what the app collects, why it collects it, who it is shared
with, and the choices available to you. It applies to the Foreword mobile app
distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store.

**Contact:** [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]

---

## 1. Summary

- The app **never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or address.**
- You can install and play without creating an account of any kind.
- When you first complete a level, the app creates an **anonymous account** — a
  randomly generated identifier with no personal details attached — so that your
  progress can be backed up and restored.
- We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** serve personalised or
  interest-based advertising.
- You can have all of your data erased at any time, on request.

---

## 2. Information the app collects

### 2.1 Account information

| What | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| An anonymous account identifier, randomly generated by our sign-in provider | To save your game progress and restore it later | Created automatically the first time you complete a level |
| A player name | Displayed in-app on your profile screen | Generated for you automatically (for example, `BOLD_BOOK_0417`); you may change it |
| A device identifier generated by the app | To distinguish your devices when restoring progress and reading diagnostic logs | On first launch |

The anonymous account identifier is **not** linked to your real-world identity.
We do not receive your email address, phone number, or any contact details when
this account is created.

The player name is chosen by you. **Please do not enter your real name or any
other personal information as your player name.** Names are limited to 3–14
characters and may contain only letters, numbers, and underscores.

### 2.2 Game progress and settings

The app stores on your device, and backs up to our servers, the information
needed to resume your game:

- Your rank, total wins, win streak, and combo counters
- Your energy count and next energy refill time
- Which daily puzzles you have started, solved, or completed, and on which dates
- Your in-progress board state and number of attempts
- Your preferences: dark theme on or off, vibration on or off, sound on or off

### 2.3 Diagnostic logs

When the app encounters a problem, it records a diagnostic entry so that the
fault can be identified and fixed. Each entry contains a timestamp, a severity
level, a short tag and message describing what the app was doing, an optional
error trace (limited to 2 KB), the app version, the runtime version, and the
app-generated device identifier described above.

These logs describe the behaviour of the software. They are not designed to
contain personal information, and we do not deliberately place any in them.

### 2.4 Advertising

The app shows **rewarded video advertisements** — short, optional videos that you
may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game hint or additional energy. You are
never required to watch an advertisement in order to play.

Advertisements are served by **Google AdMob**. The app requests
**non-personalised advertisements only**, meaning advertisements are selected
based on general context rather than on a profile of your interests or browsing
history. To deliver and measure these advertisements and to prevent fraud,
Google may process limited technical information such as your device model,
operating system version, coarse (country or region level) location derived from
your IP address, and a non-personalised advertising identifier.

Google's own privacy policy governs that processing:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 2.5 Information the app does not collect

The app does not request, access, or collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Precise or GPS location
- Your contacts, calendar, photos, or files
- Camera or microphone input — the app has no recording capability
- Health, fitness, financial, or payment information
- Your browsing history or your activity in other apps

### 2.6 Reminders and notifications

The app can send you optional reminders — for example, that a new daily puzzle
is available. These are scheduled **locally by your device** using the operating
system's own scheduler. They are not push notifications: the app does not
register a push token, does not send notification data to any server, and does
not transmit information about your playing habits in order to decide when to
remind you. Every reminder is determined on the device from information the
device already holds.

Reminders require your permission, which your device will request the first time
they are enabled. You can turn them off at any time from the app's **Settings**
screen, or withdraw the permission entirely in your device's system settings.

## 3. Optional Google sign-in

The app offers an optional feature that links your progress to a Google account,
so that you can restore it after reinstalling the app or when moving to a second
device. **This feature is entirely optional, and the app is fully functional
without it.**

If you choose to use it, Google confirms your sign-in to our sign-in provider,
and an identifier for your Google account is stored alongside your anonymous
account so that the account can be recognised on a future device. We do not
receive your Google password.

**Google accounts are subject to minimum age requirements that vary by country.
If you are below the minimum age in your country, please do not use this
feature — continue playing with the anonymous account, which requires nothing
from you.** Where a parent or guardian manages a child's Google account, for
example through Google Family Link, the parent or guardian should decide whether
this feature is used.

## 4. Children's privacy

Foreword is a word puzzle game suitable for a general audience, including
children. Our approach is as follows:

- **No personal details are ever requested.** There is no registration form, no
  email field, no profile photograph upload, and no field for contact
  information.
- **No personalised advertising.** All advertisement requests are marked as
  non-personalised and as child-directed, and advertisement content is
  restricted to a general audience ("G") rating.
- **No social features.** There is no chat, no messaging, no friend list, no
  public leaderboard, and no mechanism for players to contact one another or to
  share content.
- **No sale or sharing of data** for advertising or for any other purpose.

The identifiers we do store — the anonymous account identifier and the
app-generated device identifier — are used solely to operate the game itself:
saving your progress, restoring it, and diagnosing faults. They are not used to
build a profile of you and are not used to target advertising.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner
requiring verifiable parental consent under the United States Children's Online
Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation, or
the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

**Information for parents and guardians:** if you believe your child has
provided personal information through the app — most plausibly by typing it into
the player name field — please contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will
delete it promptly. You may also request erasure of all of your child's data
using the process described in Section 9.

## 5. How the information is used

We use the information described above only in order to:

1. Save your game progress and restore it on your device or on a new device
2. Operate the game's daily puzzle, energy, ranking, and streak systems
3. Remember your display and accessibility preferences
4. Schedule the optional reminders you have chosen to receive
5. Diagnose faults and fix problems in the app
6. Deliver the non-personalised rewarded advertisements you choose to watch
7. Protect against fraud and abuse of the advertisement reward system

We do **not** use it for profiling, for credit or eligibility decisions, or for
automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects.

## 6. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** (Article 6(1)(b)) — to provide the game and save
  your progress
- **Legitimate interests** (Article 6(1)(f)) — to diagnose faults, maintain the
  security of the app, and prevent abuse of advertisement rewards
- **Consent** (Article 6(1)(a)) — where consent is required for advertising in
  your region, obtained through Google's consent form before advertisements are
  shown, and for the optional Google sign-in feature

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not
affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

## 8. How long information is kept

We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described
in this policy. In practice:

- **Game progress and settings** are kept while your account remains in use, and
  are erased when you delete your data.
- **Diagnostic logs** are kept only as long as needed to investigate faults, and
  are routinely cleared.
- **Data stored on your device** remains until you delete your data within the
  app or uninstall the app.
- **Anonymous accounts that show no activity for an extended period** are
  removed during routine maintenance.

## 9. Your rights and choices

### Disconnecting your Google account

If you have linked a Google account, you may disconnect it at any time from your
profile screen in the app. Disconnecting signs you out on that device and stops
further backups from that device.

**Disconnecting is not deletion.** Your progress remains on the device, and the
copy already backed up to our servers is retained, so that reconnecting the same
Google account restores your game. If you want that stored copy erased, please
use the deletion request described below.

### Deleting your data

**Data on your device.** Uninstalling the app removes everything the app has
stored on your device.

**Data on our servers.** To have your stored progress, settings, and diagnostic
logs erased from our servers, email **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]** with the subject
line *Delete my data*. We will erase the records and confirm once this is done,
normally within 30 days.

Because accounts are anonymous, we need an identifier in order to find your
records. Please include the account or device identifier shown on the app's
profile screen. Without it we cannot locate your data, because we cannot
identify you from your name or email address. If you linked a Google account,
tell us which one and we can locate the records from that instead.

Deletion is permanent. Once your records are erased, your progress cannot be
restored, including by reconnecting the same Google account.

### Advertising choices

Rewarded advertisements are optional by design — simply decline to watch one.
You may also limit advertising identifiers at the operating system level:

- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → *Delete advertising ID*
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

### Your legal rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete,
restrict, or object to our processing of your information, the right to data
portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

- **EEA and UK (GDPR):** the rights listed above; complaints may be directed to
  your national data protection authority.
- **Philippines (Data Privacy Act of 2012):** the rights to be informed, to
  access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to damages, to data portability,
  and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission
  (https://privacy.gov.ph).
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** the rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out
  of the sale or sharing of personal information. **We do not sell or share
  personal information**, and we do not process sensitive personal information
  for the purpose of inferring characteristics.

To exercise any of these rights, email **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**. We will respond
within 30 days.

## 10. Security

Data held on our servers is protected by row-level security rules restricting
every record to the account that created it, so that one player cannot read
another player's data. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in
transit using TLS. Sign-in tokens are held on your device in the operating
system's secure keystore — the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain.

No system is perfectly secure. Should a breach affect your information, we will
notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by law.
